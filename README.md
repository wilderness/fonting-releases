Keep your collection in order and find the right face when you need it. Fonting helps you discover, organize, compare, and preview typefaces on your Mac.

## What Fonting does

- Browse your Mac fonts and discover open-source typefaces with clear source information.
- Organize fonts in collections, compare faces, preview every style, and edit ten full-page templates or a custom layout.
- Temporarily activate library fonts while Fonting runs, or install fonts on your Mac for use in other apps when Fonting is closed.
- Scan & Cleanup helps review categories, foundries, designers, and other font details. The analysis runs on your Mac; it does not upload font files or specimens.
- Private cloud sync backs up eligible library fonts, keeps organization in sync with the iPhone companion, and restores your library when you sign in on another Mac.
