# Fonting 0.4.0-beta.3

Fonting helps you discover, organize, preview, and compare typefaces on your Mac.

## What's new in beta.3

- More reliable link imports: discovers fonts loaded after a delay or while scrolling, including additional weights and italics.
- Supports fonts embedded in page styles and loaded directly by the website's font preview code.
- Better matching between the requested typeface, its family name, and its foundry, with fewer unrelated navigation fonts.
- Collection pages can discover fonts from their relevant linked product pages.
- All 13 supplied product and collection links passed checks for the correct typeface, usable font files, preview rendering, and temporary macOS font registration. Tested sources include MyFonts, Dinamo, Lineto, Superior Type, Dum Dum, Arillatype, Weltkern, Riptype, Regular Lines, Gruppo Due, 205TF, and FEEDTYPE.

Available styles and characters depend on what each website provides. Some sites expose only trial files or limited webfont subsets. If a site does not provide accessible font files, import a file obtained from the foundry.

## Included in the beta

- The free version holds five font families, with all styles in each family. Confirm your email to unlock unlimited families during the beta, with no password, payment details, or automatic charges.
- Cloud sync and font-file backup remain separate opt-ins.
- Preview every style at full size, compare fonts side by side or stacked, and try six square design layouts.
- New beta releases appear in an in-app toast with a Download Update button.

Beta access is not a lifetime purchase of a future paid version. Font licenses are separate from access to Fonting.

After trying to open it, if you trust this download, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway and confirm the app. Managed Macs may not permit unsigned apps. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally. Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
